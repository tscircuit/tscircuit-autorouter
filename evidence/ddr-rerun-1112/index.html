Selected fix: preserve copper layers in endpoint connectivity

DRC matched route endpoints to pads by X/Y alone. On OrangeCrab, assignable bottom-side ground pads joined eleven distinct plane nets into one DRC group, hiding conflicts and misleading repair. The fix requires a shared physical copper layer and preserves through-drill spans. Correct electrical identity is needed before repair quality can be trusted.

The passing repro uses a real 6 mm section from the new OrangeCrab run: 176 pads and 85 already-routed traces. It records one DRC net group before the fix and four after. All six false pairwise net merges are eliminated — a 100% reduction in this repro. The same snapshot path updates naturally in the stacked fix. Native copper geometry stays unchanged; colors show the groups DRC actually sees.
