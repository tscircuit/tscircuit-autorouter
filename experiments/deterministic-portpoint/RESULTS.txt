much smaller than the stage improvement because high-density routing became

Other measured configurations
----------------------------
A 16-route batch achieved 2.181x stage speedup over its original control:
22.064s original, 18.167s revised serial, 10.116s revised 4T (medians, three runs).
It also passed exact parity and graph validity checks, but one full-pipeline
check showed the downstream penalty erased the stage savings. Therefore eight
is the default despite its smaller stage-only speedup.

An exploratory density factor of 0.5 with batch 16 increased downstream repair
time substantially and was rejected. It is not used by the default prototype.

Limits
------
This was developed and tuned on one sample. The prototype changes routing
semantics and work budgets; it does not reproduce the original routing output.
It does reproduce the revised output exactly at one and four workers. At the time of these historical runs, production
pipeline files and dependency code were unchanged. The nested metrics.timeMs in
raw reports is the existing integration replay counter for the injected solver;
use top-level elapsedMs for performance comparisons.
