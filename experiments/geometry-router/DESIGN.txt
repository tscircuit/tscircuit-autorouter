Whole-board obstacle-event visibility experiment (PR 3202)

Select GeometryAutoroutingPipelineSolver explicitly. Pipeline 9 and the preserved
KrtAutoroutingPipelineSolver are separate comparators; running Pipeline 9 does
not exercise this experiment. Implementation: lib/testing/GeometryAutoroutingPipelineSolver.ts.

The engine removes capacity meshes, boundary port assignments, per-cell routing,
stitching, and post-route repair. It routes exact terminal branches on the whole
board using A* over lazily exposed obstacle events. Its candidate coordinates
come from inflated obstacle corners, pad escape events, capsule endpoints, and
original terminals. It never allocates a board-sized raster. Flatbush supplies
only collision candidates; exact segment/rotated-rectangle or segment/capsule
distances decide clearance. Circular shapes use their enclosing circle and other
nonrectangular pad types conservatively use their bounding rectangle. Through
vias check all real board layers and the original pad/drill dimensions. Same-net
copper is reusable; distinct same-net drills retain their spacing constraint.
Existing same-net through-via centers are explicit candidates and changing layer
through them carries no new-via cost. This preserves their identity and avoids
creating extra drill holes near an already usable through via.

When existing copper seals an unrouted branch, an explicit bounded order search
moves the branch before a blocking routed owner, rolls back that suffix, and
replays it. It records orders, replayed work and the last blocking-owner witness.
Unexpected errors and physical qualification errors throw. Exhaustion is a failed
solve, never a claimed partial solution or an automatic switch to Pipeline 9.
Finite candidate exhaustion is not a proof that the continuous board is unroutable.

Defaults: effort=1, viaCost=1 mm-equivalent, maxSearchNodes=20000 per branch,
maxGeometryQueries=300000 per branch, at most 32 conflict-order transactions.
Effort scales all three search budgets. Coordinate state identity rounds to 1e-7
mm; collision decisions use unrounded coordinates. This finite graph and its
state dominance rules do not provide continuous completeness or optimality.

The engine uses the existing KRT adapter's branch preparation and original-input
qualifier, without calling Rust or WASM. Unsupported features fail explicitly:
preloaded traces, blind/buried vias, via-in-pad permission, jumpers, differential
pairs/buses, typed pad/plated-hole rules, off-board connectivity and terminal-via
hints. Original connection identities, terminals, requested widths, outline and
rules remain the qualification inputs. Geometry creates arbitrary-angle segments;
angle quality, wirelength and vias must be reported, not hidden in completion.

Activation: solver.stats.backend is lazy-obstacle-visibility-v1;
pipeline9StagesExecuted must be zero. geometryQueries/searchExpansions/
obstacleEvents/noRouteStates/conflictReorders/replayedBranches measure all work.
routedBranches and throughVias count successful branch-attempt work, including
rolled-back work. acceptedBranches and acceptedThroughVias count final qualified
output. acceptedUniqueThroughVias counts distinct physical positions and
reusedViaTransitions records successful attempt transitions through existing vias.
Output getters reject unsolved states. Stage timing is exposed under
geometryVisibilitySolver, with start/end and cumulative elapsed phase time.
