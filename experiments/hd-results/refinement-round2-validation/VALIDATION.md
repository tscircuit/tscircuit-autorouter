The **completed and fully authenticated 168-record combined confirmation** `40760310372` measures this exact standalone source against control and two separate legalization sources. All raw records, pinned source/config/dependency/protocol identities and original constructor/SRJ hashes were verified, and independent summary recomputation matches. It is one exploratory observation per board and arm, without confidence intervals or equal realized co-runner guarantees.

Clean outcomes are unchanged. All **35/35 paired solved final output hashes match exactly**, including the two paired solved-but-unclean boards on dataset24. Strict completed-HD iteration/work parity holds on **36/37** boards; dataset24 sample21 changes 49,029→49,030 HD iterations and aggregate work 2,272,373→2,272,263. No claim of exact iteration/work parity on all 37 is justified. Native-worker sample01 exact-output parity remains a separately scoped correctness result.

**This is not a demonstrated significant optimization or a solve-rate improvement. Stop prioritizing lazy setup as the breakthrough path.** Shared prototypes avoid the earlier implementation’s own-property add/delete pattern, but different virtual machines and cohorts do not prove that shell overhead caused the earlier slowdown or establish a systematic speed recovery. The near-flat HD result does not justify promoting the source as a performance improvement. The dataset24 global process maxima come from different boards/stages: control is sample4 timing out in `highDensityRouteSolver` (6,493,028,352 bytes, 6.047 GiB), while lazy is sample24 timing out in `edgeSolver` before HD began (9,270,620,160 bytes, 8.634 GiB). On the matched sample24, control also times out in `edgeSolver`, with 5,098,319,872 bytes (4.748 GiB). These global maxima do not isolate HD solver memory or identify a memory effect caused by lazy setup. In particular, only the lazy global maximum is upstream of HD; both matched sample24 attempts are upstream. Timings from this confirmation and the earlier refinement are not pooled; neither does this source combine legalization with portfolio changes. These results do not establish every manufacturing rule: “clean” means the benchmark’s existing relaxed DRC.
